Risk Assessments

EV charging risk assessments you can rely on

Protect your people, your assets, and your reputation with thorough, independent risk assessments tailored specifically to EV charging infrastructure.

Why It Matters

Risk assessments are not optional

EV charging introduces unique hazards to any site. Understanding and managing those risks is not just good practice, it is a legal and commercial necessity.

Fire Safety

EV charging creates thermal risks that traditional fire safety assessments simply do not cover. Lithium ion battery incidents require specialist understanding and targeted mitigation strategies.

Compliance

UK regulations around EV charging are evolving rapidly. A comprehensive risk assessment ensures you meet current requirements and positions you ahead of upcoming changes.

Liability

If something goes wrong and you cannot demonstrate that a proper risk assessment was carried out, the liability falls squarely on the responsible person. Documented assessments are your defence.

Insurance

Insurers increasingly require evidence of EV specific risk assessments before providing or renewing cover. Without one, you may face higher premiums or gaps in your policy.

01Fire Safety

Fire Safety Checks

EV charging installations introduce thermal and electrical hazards that standard fire risk assessments do not adequately address. Our specialist fire safety checks are designed specifically for sites with charge point infrastructure, giving you clear, actionable recommendations to reduce risk.

Thermal risk evaluation assessing charge point placement, cable management, and proximity to combustible materials

Emergency response planning with EV specific protocols for fire services, staff, and site users

Actionable recommendations prioritised by severity so you know exactly where to focus resources first

Suitable for all site types including car parks, depots, commercial premises, and residential developments

04Compliance

Built around UK standards

The regulatory landscape for EV charging in the UK is complex and constantly evolving. Our risk assessments are designed to ensure you meet all current requirements and are prepared for what is coming next.

Every assessment we carry out is grounded in the latest UK regulations, industry standards, and recognised best practices, so you can be confident your compliance position is robust.

  • BS 7671 IET Wiring Regulations (18th Edition) and associated guidance notes
  • The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 as it applies to EV charging locations
  • IET Code of Practice for Electric Vehicle Charging Equipment Installation
  • Electric Vehicles (Smart Charge Points) Regulations 2021
  • OZEV grant scheme technical requirements and best practice guidance
