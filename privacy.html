Safer Charging Limited Privacy Notice

Version 1.2 Effective date 03/07/26 Last updated 03/07/26

This Privacy Notice explains how Safer Charging Limited (“Safer Charging”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data when you visit our websites or otherwise interact with us online.

This Notice applies to:

  • www.safercharging.co.uk
  • www.chargesafe.uk
  • www.arcanechargingacademy.co.uk
  • Any other public website operated by Safer Charging Limited that links to this Notice.

If you use the ChargeSafe platform or ChargeSafe mobile application, a separate ChargeSafe App Privacy Notice also applies to the processing of information collected through those services.

Nothing in this Privacy Notice affects your statutory rights under applicable data protection legislation.

Safer Charging Limited is a company registered in England and Wales.

2. What This Privacy Notice Covers

This Notice explains how we process personal information when you:

  • Visit one of our websites.
  • Contact us by email, telephone or online form.
  • Request information about our services.
  • Register for newsletters or marketing communications.
  • Create an online account where available.
  • Purchase products or services through our websites.
  • Register for training through Arcane Charging Academy.
  • Engage with us through social media or other online channels.

  • Language preferences.
  • Date and time of access.
  • Pages viewed.
  • Referring website.
  • Website navigation.
  • Error logs.

This information helps us maintain the security, performance and reliability of our websites.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our websites use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Enhance security.

Further information is available in our Cookie Policy.

We take the security of personal information seriously.

Appropriate technical and organisational measures may include:

  • Encryption.
  • Secure hosting.
  • Access controls.
  • Authentication controls.
  • Security monitoring.
  • Malware protection.
  • Firewalls.
  • Staff training.
  • Regular security reviews.

Whilst we take reasonable steps to protect personal information, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including:

  • Providing services.
  • Managing customer relationships.
  • Complying with legal obligations.
  • Resolving disputes.
  • Preventing fraud.
  • Exercising legal rights.

Retention periods vary depending upon the nature of the information, such as:

  • enquiry records, up to 3 years
  • customer records, up to 6 years after the relationship ends
  • financial records, 6 years
  • marketing preferences, until withdrawn
  • website security logs, up to 12 months

Where information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or anonymised.
