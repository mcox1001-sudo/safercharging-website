2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, tablet or mobile device when you visit a website.

Cookies allow websites to:

  • remember your preferences;
  • recognise returning users;
  • improve website functionality;
  • analyse website performance;
  • enhance security;
  • provide personalised experiences.

Some cookies remain only for your current browsing session, while others remain on your device until they expire or are deleted.

  • authentication tokens.

References to cookies within this Policy include these technologies where appropriate.

4. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation of our websites and services.

They may be used to:

  • maintain secure sessions;
  • authenticate users;
  • remember cookie preferences;
  • enable account logins;
  • protect against fraud;
  • maintain security.

These cookies cannot normally be disabled through our systems because they are required for the operation of the Services.

Functional Cookies

These cookies allow us to remember choices you make, such as:

  • language preferences;
  • accessibility settings;
  • remembered logins (where selected);
  • display preferences.

Disabling these cookies may reduce functionality.

Analytics Cookies

Subject to your consent where required, we may use analytics technologies to understand how visitors use our websites.

Analytics information may include:

  • pages visited;
  • time spent on pages;
  • navigation routes;
  • browser information;
  • device type;
  • operating system;
  • referring websites;
  • approximate location;
  • interaction with content.

This information helps us improve our websites and services.

Performance Cookies

Performance cookies help us monitor:

  • page loading speeds;
  • service reliability;
  • system performance;
  • error reporting;
  • application performance.

Marketing Cookies

With your consent, marketing cookies may be used to:

  • measure advertising effectiveness;
  • personalise advertisements;
  • prevent repeated advertisements;
  • understand campaign performance.

If you decline marketing cookies, you will continue to receive advertisements but they may be less relevant.
