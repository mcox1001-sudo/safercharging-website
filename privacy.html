Privacy Statement

Last updated 6 July 2026

Safer Charging Limited (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This statement explains, in plain English, what personal data we collect, why, and what your rights are. We are the data controller for the information described here.

What we collect

We only collect what we need to respond to you and deliver our services:

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, email address, phone number and company, when you enquire, book a Charge Point Audit or work with us.
  • Site and estate information you share so we can scope and carry out maintenance, surveys and compliance work.
  • Basic analytics about how our website is used, to help us improve it.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to enquiries, provide quotes and services, meet our legal and compliance obligations, and keep you informed where you have asked us to. We do not sell your data.

Legal basis

We rely on your consent (for example, when you contact us), the performance of a contract (to deliver services), and our legitimate interests (to run and improve our business), as set out in the UK GDPR.

Sharing

We share data only where necessary, for example with trusted suppliers who help us deliver services, or where we are legally required to. We treat client names as sensitive and reference them externally only with permission.

Keeping your data

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as required by law, and we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to keep it secure.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or ask us to delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, email hello@safercharging.co.uk. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
