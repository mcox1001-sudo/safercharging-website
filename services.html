Our Services

Comprehensive EV charging support

From proactive maintenance to independent consultancy, we cover every aspect of keeping your charge points operational, safe, and compliant.

01Core Service

Maintenance and Aftercare

Charge point aftercare just got easier. Our proactive and reactive maintenance plans are designed to minimise downtime, extend the life of your equipment, and give you peace of mind that your charging infrastructure is in expert hands.

Preventative maintenance and electrical inspection to catch issues before they cause downtime

Reactive support with 24/7 call out available UK wide with transparent SLA backed response times

Legacy estate specialists bringing older hardware back to life rather than unnecessary replacements

Hardware agnostic so we work with every manufacturer and platform on the market

Core Service
02Compliance

Risk Assessments and Compliance

As the responsible person for your site, knowing what risks are present and how to manage them effectively is essential. Our comprehensive assessments give you the clarity and documentation you need.

Fire safety checks specific to EV charging installations with actionable recommendations

PPM inspections carried out by certified EV experts to the latest standards

EICRs with detailed documentation you can present to stakeholders

Regulatory guidance helping you stay ahead of evolving UK compliance requirements

Compliance
03Advisory

Independent Consultancy

We have seen the good, the bad, and everything in between. Our years of specialist experience in EV charging mean we can reduce risk, avoid pitfalls, and guide you toward cost effective decisions that are right for your business.

Hardware and software agnostic recommendations with no ties to any manufacturer or vendor

Remote or on site assessments depending on the complexity and scale of your project

New project planning from site assessment through to equipment selection and deployment strategy

Future proofing ensuring your investment scales as your needs grow and regulations evolve

Network wide maintenance at scale with consistent quality and reporting across all your sites
