About Us

Independent EV charging experts

Safer Charging Limited is an independent maintenance and aftercare company keeping the UK's EV charging infrastructure safe, compliant, and operational.

01Our Story

Built on experience, driven by independence

Safer Charging Limited was founded with a clear purpose: to give businesses and site owners an independent, trustworthy partner for EV charging maintenance and aftercare. Based in Bedfordshire, we work with organisations across the UK who need their charge points looked after properly, without the conflicts of interest that come with manufacturer tied service providers.

We are hardware and software agnostic. That means we work with every charger brand, every back office platform, and every type of installation. Our certified EV experts bring deep technical knowledge and a genuine passion for doing things right.

As SafeContractor certified, SSIP accredited professionals and Great British Entrepreneur Awards finalists in 2025 and 2026, we hold ourselves to the highest standards in everything we do. Our clients trust us because we tell it like it is, and we always put safety and uptime first.

02Our Brand

ChargeSafe is our owned brand dedicated to raising safety standards across the EV charging industry. Through chargesafe.uk, we provide the resources, frameworks, and guidance that site owners and operators need to ensure their charging infrastructure meets the highest safety benchmarks.

Whether you are responsible for a single workplace charger or a nationwide public network, ChargeSafe gives you the tools and knowledge to protect your users, your assets, and your reputation.

Safety standards and best practice developed from real world experience maintaining charge points across the UK

Case studies

Real estates, real outcomes

Client names stay confidential, we reference them externally only with permission. The reference below is published with that permission; the engagements beneath it are real but anonymised. Further named references, with contract detail, are available to serious enquiries on request.

Blue light · Police fleet

An estate no one else would take on

27 chargers · 8 sites · mixed manufacturers

The installer and operator had gone quiet, and the manufacturers would not engage because the hardware was out of warranty. Rather than a costly full swap-out, we adopted the estate. We began with a Charge Point Audit across all eight sites, then inside a week completed back-office migration, SIM changes, new RFID cards, fuel-card integration, alongside EICR and PPM testing and a livery changeover.

Result: an uptime the fleet had never reached under the previous operator, now held with proactive and reactive maintenance and full visibility of every unit.
Commercial property

A routine assessment that wasn't

6 × 7kW · private underground car park

It began as a routine fire risk assessment, which came back largely fine. The electrical inspection did not: the chargers had been so poorly installed that the whole installation had to be isolated on safety grounds. What looked like a few hundred pounds of routine work became a proper rectification.

Result: an estate that is now genuinely safe and compliant, not merely certified, and the clearest reason we pair the inspection with the fix.
Fleet operator · Scaling

Support that scales with the network

~150 → several thousand sockets

A fleet charging operator growing fast needed dependable first and second-line response as it scaled. We put in our Core Service Plan: intelligent proactive and reactive maintenance kept to minimal touchpoints, an annual inspection plus a lighter six-monthly RCD check, and a per-socket model where the unit cost falls as the network grows.

Result: predictable cost as they expand, with support treated as a technical discipline rather than a cost centre.
Blue light · Second force

Repeat work in a sector that's hard to win

Fire risk & site assessment · estate-wide

Fire risk and site assessment across a second police estate, with a maintenance programme now in the pipeline. Blue-light buyers do not hand work out lightly, and they do not stay unless the delivery holds up.

Result: a live relationship and repeat sector work, evidence that the standard travels from one estate to the next.
