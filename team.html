Founder and CEO
01Leadership

Chris Cheetham

Founder and Chief Executive Officer

Chris founded Safer Charging with a clear mission: to create an independent, trustworthy partner for EV charging maintenance and aftercare. With extensive experience in the EV charging industry, he recognised that too many charge point owners were left without reliable support once installation was complete.

His vision for the company centres on independence. By remaining hardware and software agnostic, Safer Charging can serve every client without the conflicts of interest that come with manufacturer tied service providers. Under Chris's leadership, the company has secured SafeContractor certification, SSIP accreditation, and a place as finalists in the Great British Entrepreneur Awards 2025.
