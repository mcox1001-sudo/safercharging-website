Common Questions

Frequently asked

Answers to the questions we hear most often about our service plans.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or adjust your plan at any time as your charging estate grows. We will simply pro rata the difference and update your cover straight away.

Do you work with all charger brands?

Yes. We are completely hardware agnostic and work across every major manufacturer and platform. Whether you have a single brand estate or a mixed portfolio, we have you covered.

What areas do you cover?

We provide coverage across the whole of the UK. For Enterprise customers we can arrange dedicated regional engineers for faster response times.

Are parts and materials included?

Standard consumables and common parts are included in Professional and Enterprise plans. For larger component replacements, we provide transparent pricing with no hidden markups.

What is proactive monitoring?

We combine a non-invasive audit with preventative maintenance and electrical inspection to catch issues before they cause downtime. That means fewer reactive call outs and better uptime for your estate.

Is there a minimum contract period?

Our standard plans run on a 12 month rolling agreement. Enterprise plans are flexible and can be tailored to match your procurement cycles. We are always happy to discuss what works best for you.
