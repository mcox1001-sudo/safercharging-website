Why we exist

Most charge point problems start when the people who installed the hardware move on. We pick it up and keep it running.

For fleets, workplaces, property owners and public sector bodies that depend on EV charging, Safer Charging is the maintenance and compliance specialist that keeps charging estates safe, compliant and working. Unlike generalist installers who treat maintenance as a sideline, it is all we do.
